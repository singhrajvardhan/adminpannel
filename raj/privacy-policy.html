1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Better Growth Solutions ("Company", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and processes the personal information you provide during the employee onboarding process. By submitting your information through our onboarding portal, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

2. Information We Collect

During the onboarding process, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Full name, father's name, mother's name, date of birth
  • Contact Information: Primary mobile number, alternate mobile numbers, email address, current address
  • Identity Documents: PAN card, Aadhaar card (front & back)
  • Photographs: Candidate photograph, house photographs, street photographs
  • Educational Documents: 10th marksheet, 12th marksheet, graduation & post-graduation certificates (if applicable)
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) captured during onboarding
  • Resume/CV: Your uploaded resume document

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Employment verification and background checks
  • Creating and maintaining your employee profile
  • Payroll processing and salary disbursement
  • Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Managing attendance, shifts, and work schedules
  • Verifying identity and preventing fraud
  • Communicating with you regarding your employment
  • Internal quality assurance and audit purposes

4. Data Storage & Protection

All personal data collected is stored securely on our servers with restricted access. Sensitive documents (such as PAN and Aadhaar) are encrypted using industry-standard encryption methods before storage.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only when required:

  • By law or in response to a legal request
  • With regulatory authorities when mandated
  • With trusted service providers who assist in payroll, verification, or IT operations (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • In the event of a company merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you are employed with the company, and for a reasonable period thereafter as required by law or for legitimate business purposes. After the retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Rights

As a user of our onboarding system, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time (may affect employment)

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@bettergrowthsolutions.com
